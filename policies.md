# Retail Credit Risk Policy Manual (Internal)

## POL-001 Debt-to-Income Threshold
Total monthly debt obligations, including the proposed EMI, must not exceed 50% of
verified gross monthly income. Applications between 40% and 50% DTI require a
compensating factor (credit score above 750, or collateral coverage above 120%).
Applications above 50% DTI are declined at the analyst level and cannot be
overridden without Credit Committee approval.

## POL-002 Minimum Credit Score
Unsecured personal loans require a bureau score of 700 or above. Scores between
650 and 699 may be approved only with a co-applicant or with the loan amount
capped at 60% of the requested principal. Scores below 650 are declined.

## POL-003 Employment Stability
Salaried applicants must show at least 12 months of continuous employment with the
current employer and 24 months of total work history. Self-employed applicants must
provide 3 years of income tax returns showing positive net income in at least 2 of
the 3 years.

## POL-004 Loan-to-Value on Secured Lending
LTV must not exceed 80% for residential property, 70% for commercial property, and
60% for unlisted securities. Valuations older than 90 days are not accepted.

## POL-005 Existing Delinquency
Any account currently 90+ days past due results in automatic decline. A history of
30-59 DPD in the last 12 months requires escalation to a senior analyst. Written-off
or settled accounts within the last 24 months require Credit Committee review.

## POL-006 Exposure Concentration
Aggregate exposure to a single borrower may not exceed 10% of Tier 1 capital.
Unsecured exposure to a single retail borrower is capped at INR 25,00,000.

## POL-007 KYC and Documentation
Approval is contingent on complete KYC. Income proof must be no older than 3 months.
Bank statements covering 6 months are mandatory for all applicants.

## POL-008 Fair Lending and Adverse Action
Decline decisions must state the specific principal reason in writing. Decisions may
not reference protected attributes. The applicant must be informed of the right to
obtain their bureau report used in the decision.

## POL-009 Risk-Based Pricing
Band A (score 780+, DTI under 30%): benchmark rate. Band B (720-779): benchmark
+ 150 bps. Band C (700-719 or DTI 30-40%): benchmark + 300 bps. Band D: decline or
secured conversion only.

## POL-010 Age and Tenure Limits
Applicant age at maturity of the loan must not exceed 65 years for salaried and 70
years for self-employed applicants. Maximum tenure for unsecured lending is 60 months.
